# SCAR (severe cutaneous adverse drug reaction)
- เเบ่งเป็น
	- Immediate reaction = 1-12 hr
		- Urticaria
		- Anaphylaxis
	- Delay reaction
		- Maculopapular drug eruption = 4-14 d
		- AGEP = 1-12 d
		- SJS/TEN = 4-28 d
		- DRESS = 2-8 wk
## Clinical feature
### SJS (steven-johnson syndrome) / TEN (toxic epidermal necrolysis)
- S/S 
	- Fever
	- Rash at mucosal tissue
	- Dusky macules
		![[Screenshot 2568-12-17 at 23.57.30.png|300]]
	- Eye mouth genital pain
- Body area
	- SJS < 10%
	- TEN > 30%
		![[image-26.png]]
### DRESS (drug-induced hypersensitivity syndrome / drug rash with eos and systemic symptom)
- S/S
	- Fever
	- Diffuse deep red morbiliform eruption with <span class='hl1'>facial involvement</span>
		![[Screenshot 2568-12-17 at 23.58.39.png|300]]
	- Multiple organ involvement eg. hepatitis, lymphadinitis, nephritis
### AGEP (acute generalized exanthematous pustulosis)
- S/S
	- Fever
	- Pinpoint pustules overlying erythematous eruption
