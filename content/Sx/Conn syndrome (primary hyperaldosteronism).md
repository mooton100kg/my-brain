# Pathophysiology
- Aldosterone ↑↑ 
	- ↑ renal Na reabsorption = hypertension
	- ↑ renal K secretion = hypokalemia → metabolic alkalosis
- Aldosterone escepe = ทำให้ conn syndrome ไม่มี edema & hypernatremia
	- Na & water retention
	- ANP secretion → ↓ Na & water
# Clinical feature
- <span class='hl1'>2º hypertension</span> = ไม่ตอบสนองต่อ anti-hypertension
- Hypokalemia
	- Muscle weakness
	- Polyuria = hypokalemia → desensitize to ADH
	- Palpitation
# Management
- Adrenal hyperplasia = aldosterone receptor antagonist
	- Spironolactone
	- Eplerenone
- Aldosteronoma = surgery
- Hypertension = steroid
