# Criteria
- Low muscle mass
	- BIA
		- Male < 7 kg/m2
		- Female < 5.7 kg/m2
	- DXA
		- Male < 7 kg/m2
		- Female < 5.4 kg/m2
- <span class='hl3'>or</span> Low muscle strength
	- Hand grip strength
		- Male < 28kg
		- Female < 18kg
- <span class='hl3'>or</span> Low physical performance 
	- 5-time chair stand ≥ 12 sec
	- 6-metre walk < 1 m/sec
