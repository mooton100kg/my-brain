- Lesion = skin-colored dome-shaped papule with central umbilication
	![[image-16.png|200]]
- Investigation = Wright's stain → intracytoplasmic inclusion body
- Management
	- Salicylic acid

## HSV1,2 infection
- Lesion = vesicle at lip, gum, mucosa
	![[image-17.png|300]]
	- ติดเชื้อครั้งเเรก = primary herpetic gingivostomatitis
	- Cervical lymphadenopathy
- Investigation = Tzanck smear → multinucleated giant cell
- Management
	- Xylocaine viscous
	- In immunocompromised = acyclovir
