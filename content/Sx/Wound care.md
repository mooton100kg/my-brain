3. Prevent desiccation
4. Gas exchangeable
5. Barrier to microorganism
6. Prevent contamination

# Wound complication
## Seroma
### Definition
- Lymphatic fluid under incision wound
	- เย็บไม่ถึงก้นเเผล
	- เลาะ LN เเล้วไม่ได้ใส่ drainage
### S/S
- Well-circumscribed swelling → clear liquid drainage
- US = clear black anechoic
### Prevention
- Suction drainage
### Mx
- Aspiration
- Open drainage (infection)
- Pressure dressing 
## Hematoma
### Definition
- Collection of blood in SC
	- Inadequate hemostasis
	- Coagulopathy
### S/S
- Purplish blue discoloration
- Localized soft swelling
- Complication = compartment syndrome
- <span class='hl4'>มีโอกาสเกิด infection มากกว่า seroma</span>
- US = hypoechoic ± septation ± heterogenicity
### Prevention
- Collected coagulopathy
	![[Screenshot 2568-12-24 at 21.54.54.png|189x215]]
- Adequate hemostasis intra-op
### Mx
- Correct coagulopathy
- Conservative
- Indication for evacuation (drain + repair damage)
	- Large / expanding hematoma
	- Unstable patient
## Dehiscence (acute wound failure)
### Definition
- เเผลไม่ติดกัน ส่วนใหญ่เกิด post-op 7-10d
	- Technical error
	- Emergency surgery
	- Intra-abdominal infection
	- Advance age
	- Wound infection seroma/hematoma
	- High IAP
	- Obesity
	- Chronic steroid used
	- Malnutrition
	- **Post radiation / chemo**
	- Systemic dz (DM, SLE)
### S/S
- Superficial = intact rectus sheath
- Deep = rectus sheath fail ± evisceration (protrusion of content)
	![[Screenshot 2568-12-22 at 00.25.32.png|176x229]]
### Prevention
- Careful fascia closure, tension-free
- Retention suture
- Avoiding heavy lifting
- Nutrition
### Mx
- Initial = remove suture + ใช้นิ่วตรวจดู restus sheath
- Wound care with saline-moisted gauze packing
- Abdominal binder
- Evisceration
	- Cover bowel with sterile saline-moisted towel <span class="hl4">= keep moise + temp</span>
	- IV resuscitation
	- Broad spectrum IV ATB
	- Urgent re-operation
## Surgical site infection
### Pathogen
- <span class='hl1'>Staphylococcus aureus</span>
- Enterococcus
- Candida
- E. coli
- Pseudomonas
### S/S
- Erythema, tenderness, edema
- Abscess = soft + fluctuant

- Superficial = purulent drainage
- Deep = abscess in deep space
- Organ space = purulent drainage from organ space
### Prevention
- Stop smoking 2wk <span class="hl4">(ถ้าลด RS event จะเป็น 4wk)</span>
- Nutrition
- ATB prophylaxis = cefazolin ยกเว้น Uro ใช้ fq
### Management
- Explore wound
- Drain pus
- Superficial / deep
	- Debridement 
	- NSS irrigation
	- Wet dressing
- Organ space
	- ± Percutaneous drainage / re-op
### Wound classification
- Clean = elective procedure
- Clean-contaminated = hollow organ
- Coontaminated = open trauma
- Dirty = หนอง
## Post operative fever
- 24hr post-op = inflammation
- 48-72hr post-op = atelectasis
- >72hr post-op = infection

- Caues = 6W
	- Wind (lung)
		- Atelectasis 
		- Pneumonia
	- Wound (infection)
	- Water (UTI)
	- Waste (GI obs)
	- Wonder drug (ATB)
	- Walker (thrombosis)
### Atelectasis & pneumonia Tx
- Stop smoking 1wk before Sx
- Adequate pain control
- Incentive spirometer
- Proper ETT care
- Early mobilization
