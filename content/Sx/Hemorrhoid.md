- Definition = dilation of vascular plexus in anal cushion
	- Internal / external = ตัดที่ dentate line

> [!info] Anal cushion
> Maintain anal continence

- M/C location = 3 / 7 / 11 o'clock

- Pathophysiology
	- Loss of fixation
	- Vascular abnormality
# Clinical feature
## History
- Rectal bleeding
- Perianal pain
- Pruritus
- Prolapse
## Physixal exam
- PR + proctoscope = sims' position
	- ไม่ทำถ้ามี anal pain ให้เปลี่ยนไปทำ under anesthesia เเทน
### Grading
- 1 = bleeding / no prolapse
- 2 = prolapse + reduce spontaneously
- 3 = prolapse + manual reduction
- 4 = prolapse + can not reduce

# Management
- Life-style modificaiton = กินน้ำ high fiber diet
- Medical = grade 1-2
	- Topical steroid
	- Stool softener
	- Flavonoid = **daflon**
- Ambulatory = grade 2
	- Rubber band ligation = ต้องเป็น internal (above dentate line)
	- Sclerotherapy
- Surgical = grade 3-4
	- Hemorrhoidectomy
## <span class='hl1'>Indication for sx</span> #imo

	- Internal = ผ่าเลย
- Grade 4
- Concomitant anal condition
- Bleeding
- Convined hemorrhoid
