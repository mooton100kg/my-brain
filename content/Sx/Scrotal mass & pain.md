# Hydrocele
- Definition = accumulation of fluid in tunica vaginalis
- Classification
	- Hydrocele of cord = defective closure of processus vaginalis
	- Congenital communicating hydrocele = failure of closing processus vaginalis
	- Non-communicating hydrocele = defect in tunica vaginalis
## S/S
- Painless scrotal mass
- Sensation of heaviness fullness draging
- Scrotal enlargement<br>
- Transillumination test positive
## Investigaiton
- Inguinal-scrotal US = เเยก testicular CA ที่สร้างน้ำออกมา
## Management
- Indication of surgery
	- Failure of hydrocele to resolve spontaneously after interval of observation
	- Association of hydrocele with suggestive pathology <span class="hl4">(eg. torsion, tumor)</span>
	- Pain / discomform
	- Patient desire
- Hydrocelectomy
	- Children = inguinal approach
	- Adult = scrotal approach

# Varicocele
- Definition = dilatation of pampiniform venous plexus & internal spermatic vein
- Classification
	- 1 = palpable only with valsalva
	- 2 = palpable not visible
	- 3 = visible
- M/C location = Lt.
	- Lt. testicular vein เข้า Lt. renal vein ก่อน
	- Nutcracker effect = Lt. renal vein โดน superior mesenteric artery กด
		![[Screenshot 2568-12-21 at 15.06.46.png|300]]
	- ถ้าเจอข้าง Rt. ต้องหา retroperitoneal mass
## S/S
- Aching pain in scrotum หลังยืนหรือเดินนานๆ
- Infertility
- Bag of worm
## Investigation
- US = vessel of pampiniform plexus > 3mm + reversal of flow
## Management
- Indication of sx
	- Pain / discomfort = หลังผ่าโอกาสหายปวด 50%
	- Infertility
- Varicocelectomy
