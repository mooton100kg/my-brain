# Cause
- Parainfluenza virus
- Onset = 6mo - 3yo
# Clinical feature
## History
- ไข้ ไอ น้ำมูกนำมาก่อน
- Barking cough
- เสียงเเหบ
## PE
- Stridor
- Retraction, cyanosis
## Investigation
- AP view = steeple sign
- Lateral neck x-ray = ballooning hypopharynx
	![[Screenshot 2569-01-23 at 00.03.33.png|300]]

> [!tip] Ddx with upper airway obs
> - Bacterial tracheitis
> - Acute epiglottitis
> - FB aspiration
> - Retropharyngeal abscess
# Grading (Downes score)
- mild < 4 
- Mod 4-7
- Severe > 7
<span class='hl1' style="font-size: 1.5em">ไอๆ stridor ร้องก็เขียว เดี๋ยวเดียว retract เเค๊กๆ breath sound</span>
![[Screenshot 2569-01-23 at 00.00.59.png]]
# Management
- Nebulized Epi (1:1000) 0.5 ml/kg 
	- Max 
		- <4 yo = 2.5 ml
		- >4 yo = 5 ml
- Dexamethasone 0.6 mgKg IV
	- Max 10 mg
- ETT
	- ใส่ขนาดเล็กกว่าที่คำนวณ 0.5-1 mm

> [!info] ETT size
> Cuff = age/4 + 4
> Uncuff = age/4 + 4.5
