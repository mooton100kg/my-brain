# Surgical drain
## Indication
- Theraputic
	- Drain fluid collection from body
	- Drain air from pleural cavity
	- Drain ascites, chyle, bile from abdominal cavity
- Prophylactic
	- Remove excess serum, prevent hematoma
	- Early detection of anastomosis leakage
	- Prevent stricture
