# Clinical feature
## History
- Pain while passing stool
- Passing fresh blood 
- Bad smell D/C
## Physical exam
- PR under sedation
### Chronic grading
- 1 = sentinel pile
- 2 = hypertrophic anal papillae
- 3 = exposed internal sphincter
- 4 = fibrotic edge
# DDx
- Crohn disease = multiple ulcer
- Syphilis = broad ulcer, indurated
- Tuberculosis = superficial ulcer, granulated wound bed with purulent 
- Anal carcinoma = depigmentation, necrotic
- HIV, CMV, HSV, leukemia, histoplasmosis, leishmaniasis
