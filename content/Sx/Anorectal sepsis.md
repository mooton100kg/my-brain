- Anorectal abscess = anal abscess 
- Anal fistula = small tunnel from anal canal to outside skin
	- ~ 50% ของคนที่เป็น abscess จะกลายเป็น fistula

> [!info] Fournier gangrene
> Severe NF of perineal area

# Clinical feature
## History
- Swollen inflamed bump
- Pus-like D/C
- Bleeding
# Management
- Surgery drainage
	- Sphincter preserving
		- **Ligation of sphincteric fistulous tract**
		- Endorectal & anodermal advancement flap
		- Anal fistula plug
		- Drainage & loose seton
	- Non-sphincter preserving 
		- Simple fistulotomy
		- Fistulectomy
		- Cutting seton
