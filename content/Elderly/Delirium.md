# Management
- Review ยาทุกตัว โดยเฉพาะ BZD & anti-cholinergic
- Control pain
- Treat infection
- Treat constipation
- Promote sleep cycle
- Re-orientation
- If alcohol & BZD withdrawal = ให้ benzodiazepine
