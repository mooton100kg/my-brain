# Screening for falling risk
- Timed up and go ≥ 15sec
- 30sec sit to stand ≤ 10
- 4 stage balance test ≤ 10sec
	- เท้าชิด → semi-tandem → tandem → one-leg
- Gait speed ≤ 0.8 m/sec
# Management to reduce fall risk
## Exercise
- Resistance training
- Balance & gait exercise
- Dual task exercise
- Tai chi
- Dance intervention
