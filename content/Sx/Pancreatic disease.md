# Acute biliary pancreatitis
- Cause
	- GS at distal CBD
	- Alcohol
## S/S
- Acute onset persistent severe epigastrium pain
- Radiate to back
- โน้มตัวไปข้างหน้าเเล้วดีขึ้น
## Criteria diagnosis (Atlanta 2013) (≥2)
- Typical abdominal pain
- Serum lipase / amylase ≥ 3x UNL = amylase จะขึ้นเร็วลงเร็ว กว่า lipase
	![[Pasted image 20251103022537.png|200]]
- Characteristic finding of acute pancreatitis on contrast-enhanced CT
	- Pancreatic swelling
	- Peripancreatic fluid / fat

- <span class='hl2'>ถ้า lipase / amylase สูงไม่มาก ให้สงสัย</span>
	- Hollow viscus perforation
	- Acute cholecystitis
	- Mesenteric ischemia

- Organ failure (modified marshall score ≥ 2)
	![[Pasted image 20251103024134.png|300]]

- Early treatment
	- Initial assessment
		- Hemodynamic status
		- Risk assessment
	- Supportive care
		- Fluid resus
		- Pain control & antiemetic
		- Nutrition support within 72hr
	- Monitoring for complication
		- MAP 65
		- Urine output > 0.5 ml/kg/hr
- Specific treatment
	- ERCP
		- Indication
			- Concomittant cholangitis
			- Persistent obs jaundice
	- Cholecystectomy
		- ทำใน Mild pancreatitis after ERCP
			- Same admission
		- ไม่ทำใน severe pancreatitis 
	- Pancreatic necrosectomy = ถ้ามี infected pancreatic necrosis
